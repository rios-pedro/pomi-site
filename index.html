Features

Built to disappear
into your workflow.

Every decision in Pomi favors calm over cleverness.

Menu bar only

No dock icon, no window clutter. Pomi opens where you need it and disappears the moment you don't.

A ring, not a number

Time is shown as a shape that shrinks, not just digits that count down. You feel the session ending before you read it.

Presets that fit

5, 15, 25, 45 — or drag the slider to anything in between. No setup screens, no settings to dig through.

Native notifications

A single, quiet system notification when time's up. No sound you didn't choose, no pop-up you didn't ask for.

Closes on its own

Click anywhere outside, or switch apps — the window hides itself. You never have to think about closing it.

Native and light

Built with Tauri, not Electron. Pomi starts instantly and stays out of your battery's way.
